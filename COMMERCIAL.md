# Commercial licensing

Nudge1's code is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). That license is
free for anyone — individuals, small teams, agencies running it for
clients — including running a modified version as a hosted service, as long
as you release the source of your modified version to the people using it
(that's what AGPL requires that a plain open-source license like MIT doesn't).

**If you're a larger organization** — as a rough guide, more than 25
employees or over $1M in annual revenue — and you want to use Nudge1 (as-is
or modified, including as part of a paid product or internal tool) without
the AGPL's source-disclosure obligation, you need a separate commercial
license instead. Reach out via [github.com/mike1ros-gh](https://github.com/mike1ros-gh)
to arrange one.

This file states a policy, not a signed contract — it's not a substitute for
an actual licensing agreement, and the thresholds above are a starting point
for a conversation, not a precise legal test. If you're relying on this to
decide whether your organization is compliant, get your own legal advice
first.
